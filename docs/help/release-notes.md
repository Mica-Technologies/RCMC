# Release notes

Releases are numbered by date. Downloads, with checksums, are on the
[GitHub releases page](https://github.com/Mica-Technologies/RCMC/releases). Pre-releases are published
for every change and cleared out after 90 days; the full releases below are the ones to use.

## 2026.09.23 — first release

The first public release of RCMC, being tried out first in the **Alto_EXPERIMENTAL** pack.

**Coasters**

- Track laid as smooth curves through the nodes you place, with the **Track Builder**, or from a palette
  of ready-made pieces with the **Piece Builder**: straights, slopes, curves, helices, airtime hills and
  vertical loops, and heartline **inversions** (corkscrew, zero-g roll, Immelmann and dive loop).
- Hardware: chain lifts, launches forwards and backwards, brake runs, block brakes, drive tyres,
  transfer tracks and stations.
- Stations with platforms and **air gates**, boarding only in the station, and restraints out on the
  course.
- The **operator panel**: open, test, close, emergency stop, manual dispatch, trains and hardware
  tuning. **Ride signs**, ride names, and RCT-style excitement, intensity and nausea **ratings**.
- The **ride check**: where riders would feel too much, and where a train can't get past, marked on the
  track while you edit it.
- **Block sections** to run several trains; **shuttle coasters**; **storage** by transfer table.
- The **Track Editor** for changing track you've already built, with undo and redo.
- A rider's camera that rolls with the car, a ride HUD with speed and G, G-force screen effects, and
  sounds for rolling, lifts, brakes, launches, dispatch, gates and wind.

**Metro**

- Self-driving trains in three sizes, with doors, cabs, lamps, and signs inside and out.
- Stations with one or more platforms, and doors that open on the platform side.
- Lines that loop, shuttle, or turn back on turning loops; switches for junctions.
- Trains slow for curves, hold their brakes at platforms, and never run into each other; signals to
  space them out, placed with the **Transit Builder**.
- Arrival boards, line-map signs and station speakers, and spoken announcements with City Super Mod.
- The **Line Control Desk** to run a line: add, remove and hold trains, set dwell and headway.
- **Build checks** for slow curves, steep grades and awkward platforms.
